About RideSync

Share Seats, Share Safety. Ride Together, Reach Together.

Campus Commuting

RideSync helps college students travelling along the same route connect for daily home-to-campus and campus-to-home trips using structured pickup and drop points.

Ride Sharing

Students with registered vehicles can offer empty seats on upcoming rides, review incoming seat requests, and manage bookings in one place.

Fair Cost Sharing

Drivers set a clear price per seat when posting a ride, and seekers see the exact total fare calculation before sending a ride request.

How RideSync Works

For Ride Seekers

Browse upcoming active rides or search by pickup location, destination, date, preferred time, and required seats. Matching rides are ranked by time proximity and driver ratings. Send a request, track approval, view driver contact and vehicle details once booked, and rate completed rides.

For Ride Givers

Register your vehicle, post rides with available passenger seats and per-seat pricing, accept or reject incoming requests with automatic seat updates, and view feedback from fellow students.
