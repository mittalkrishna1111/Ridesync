Platform Safety & Trust

Built-in account, booking, and moderation controls in RideSync.

College Account Registration

Users register with their full name, College ID, email address (Gmail or Bennett University email), and a 10-digit phone number, with duplicate checks enforced in the database.

Account Lockout & Status Controls

Accounts are protected against repeated failed login attempts (temporary 15-minute lockout after 5 incorrect attempts for users) and can be marked ACTIVE, BLOCKED, or INACTIVE by administrators.

Driver Approval & Vehicle Records

Every ride is tied to a registered vehicle with a unique registration number and seating capacity. Drivers explicitly accept or reject ride requests before a booking is confirmed.

Transparent Ratings & Admin Oversight

Ride seekers can submit 1–5 star ratings and comments for completed rides, and administrators can review reports, monitor active rides, manage accounts/vehicles, and inspect audit records.
